# Finding: the "failure rate" behind the frozen route payout

**To:** Dashboard team 
**From:** Trust and Data

**What the dashboard said, and what the data shows.** The dashboard reported that about half of all bookings end as a passenger no-show, and on that basis the route profitability payout was frozen. When we went back to the booking records, we could not find anything close to that. Of 200 bookings, 156 (78%) were confirmed and travelled normally. Another 34 (17%) were cancelled, and only 12 of those, or 6% of all bookings, were cancelled because the passenger did not show up. A further 10 bookings (5%) are under dispute. Even if every cancelled and disputed booking is counted as a problem, the total is 22%, still well under half. The route manager who questioned the number was right.

**Where the error came from.** We found two ways the dashboard's logic can quietly give the wrong answer, and neither produces any error message. First, most bookings (166 of 200) have no cancellation reason on file, simply because nothing went wrong with them. A filter such as "reason is not X" silently skips every booking where the reason is blank, so a large share of the healthy bookings never gets counted in whichever total or share the dashboard shows. Second, bookings are linked to routes in a way that repeats a booking once for every route its flight serves: joining them produces 385 lines from 200 real bookings, and flights with no route listed disappear altogether. Either problem can distort a rate without anyone noticing. One caveat: we were not given the dashboard's exact query, so we could not reproduce the "about half" figure itself. We can say the figure is not supported by the booking data, and that these two weaknesses are in exactly the places where it could have gone wrong.

**What should happen to the payout.** We recommend the freeze be lifted, or at minimum not be renewed on the strength of this number. The data shows a no-show rate of about 6%, which does not on its own justify holding back a route profitability payout. Before any further decision, the dashboard team should send us the original query so we can confirm exactly where "half" came from, rerun the figure with blanks and repeated bookings handled correctly, and have the corrected result reviewed by the route manager who raised the concern. Any future dashboard showing a rate should be checked this way before it is used to hold money.
